Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: One cohesive concept-art review sheet for a proposed independent Minecraft conveyor-and-inserter mod. This is exploratory artwork, not a screenshot, texture atlas, finished game asset, or evidence of implementation.
Primary request: Show an original, practical Minecraft-compatible conveyor and compact mechanical inserter family, with chunky cuboid geometry, restrained low-resolution pixel textures, iron framework and dark rubber belt surfaces. Match the visual simplicity of vanilla Minecraft and classic industrial mods without copying another game's machines. Single center lane, readable direction markings, no high-tech glowing decorations.
Composition: Landscape design review board on a light neutral background, four clearly separated large panels, consistent isometric camera, generous spacing, small legible panel labels only:
"01 STRAIGHT + TURN" — a one-block-wide, low-profile straight belt and a separate right-angle belt. Pixel-textured iron edges, rubber slats, a few Minecraft-style iron ingots spaced along the center lane.
"02 SIDE MERGE" — a straight main belt continues in one direction, a perpendicular side belt ends into its side in a T junction. Main line is visibly continuous. Side items wait at the junction behind a gap in main line traffic. No four-way intersection, no independent crossing lane.
"03 INSERTER" — two parallel single-lane belts, a small cuboid pedestal and articulated blocky arm with a two-prong gripper between them, reaching from belt A toward belt B carrying one iron ingot. Clear pick and place reach, no portal, no pipe.
"04 RAMP" — one belt ascends one Minecraft block height across a plausible diagonal ramp connecting a lower and an upper horizontal run; block grid shown by a few simple support cubes. No impossible collision, no crossing another belt.
Materials/textures: Pixel-painted surfaces with visibly coarse texels, simple un-beveled cuboid parts, no photorealistic scratches, no smooth sci-fi robots. Functional proportions at Minecraft block scale. Direction arrows may be light paint, but keep one consistent belt direction per run.
Text: top small title "LOGISTICS / CONCEPT 01", bottom small caption "VISUAL STUDY — NOT IN-GAME".
Constraints: One art direction only. No character, no logos, no brand name, no dense UI, no floating explanatory paragraphs, no same-level non-mixing belt crossing. Do not imply an approved block footprint or final mechanical animation. All four panels must be individually readable.
